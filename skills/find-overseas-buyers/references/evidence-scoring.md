# Buyer evidence scoring

Score each candidate out of 100. Record the evidence, not just the number.

## Product fit — 40 points

- 35–40: official catalog shows products that directly consume the supplied material/component;
- 25–34: official site confirms closely matching manufacturing or importing activity;
- 10–24: distributor or brand with a plausible downstream need;
- 0–9: only an industry-directory category or weak keyword match.

## Purchasing evidence — 30 points

- 25–30: current tender, request for quotation, sourcing notice, import record, or explicit supplier request;
- 15–24: documented production capacity, active expansion, repeated imports, or published supplier onboarding;
- 5–14: continuing product sales imply recurring consumption, but no procurement evidence;
- 0–4: no demand evidence beyond broad company relevance.

## Contactability — 20 points

- 16–20: verified procurement/sourcing contact or official business email plus phone/form;
- 10–15: official general email or working contact form;
- 5–9: only a business phone or social business account;
- 0–4: directory-only or unverifiable contact.

## Freshness — 10 points

- 8–10: evidence updated within 12 months;
- 5–7: evidence within 36 months or undated but clearly current official site;
- 1–4: older or ambiguous evidence;
- 0: stale, unavailable, or contradicted.

## Demand labels

- `Confirmed`: direct, current purchasing evidence or confirmation from the company.
- `Probable`: strong product fit and business activity, but no direct purchase request.
- `Unverified`: plausible fit with insufficient primary evidence. Keep only when clearly labeled and when stronger candidates are unavailable.
